Official HCID Video Training Library

Scan the QR codes with your mobile device or click the links below to watch official step-by-step video guides produced by the UK HCID Training Network and Health & Safety Executive (HSE).

🎥 1. Unified HCID PPE Donning Guide

Demonstrates the step-by-step procedure for putting on the UK Unified HCID Assessment PPE Ensemble, including buddy checklist inspection, respirator fit check, and glove taping.

▶ Watch Donning Video ↗

🎥 2. Unified HCID PPE Doffing Guide

Demonstrates the critical step-by-step removal of contaminated HCID PPE in the Amber Zone under direct buddy direction, highlighting the No-Touch policy and hand hygiene steps.

▶ Watch Doffing Video ↗

🎥 3. Clinical Sample Handling & Category A Packaging

Demonstrates blood sampling, double bagging with absorbent gauze, placement in protective plastic secondary containers, and sealing inside the UN Class 6.2 Category A transport box for RIPL.

▶ Watch Sample Video ↗

🎥 4. UK HCID Network Resource Centre

Access the full repository of UK HCID simulation videos, paediatric clinical guidance, UV contamination models, posters, and UKHSA clinical pathways.

🌐 Visit Resource Centre ↗
