High Consequence Infectious Diseases (HCID)

NEW INTERACTIVE DIGITAL CLINICAL STAFF TRAINING GUIDE & ASSESSMENT

Aneurin Bevan University Health Board has launched a new interactive digital training resource to support clinical staff preparedness for High Consequence Infectious Diseases (HCID). This course aligns with national guidance from HCID-UK, UKHSA, and Public Health Wales.

📚 Course Curriculum

  • Module 1: Intro to HCID Governance
  • Module 2: Contact HCIDs (Ebola, Lassa, CCHF)
  • Module 3: Airborne HCIDs (MERS, Avian Flu)
  • Module 4: Doctor Action Cards & Pathway
  • Module 5: Patient Assessment & Pathway
  • Module 6: Unified HCID PPE Ensemble
  • Module 7: Donning & Doffing Buddy System
  • Module 8: Enhanced Zoning (Red/Amber/Green)
  • Module 9: Category A Diagnostic Sampling
  • Module 10: Clinical Decision Scenarios

🎥 Interactive Video Guides

  • Official step-by-step PPE Donning Video.
  • Official step-by-step PPE Doffing Video.
  • Step-by-step Category A Sample Handling Video.
  • Scannable QR codes for mobile devices.
  • Opens in a separate browser tab to preserve course progress.
